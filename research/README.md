# Research & reading list

A growing, **community-curated** bibliography for building safe, ethical AI and the reputation systems that hold it accountable. This is a starting scaffold — most of it should come from *you*.

## How to add an entry (PRs welcome)
One line per entry, in the relevant section:
> **Title** — Authors (Year). [link]. *One sentence on why it matters here.*

Primary sources preferred; give a real, checkable citation (no "someone showed that…"). If a paper, preprint, or idea shaped how you think about **trust, reputation, verification, incentives, or honesty in AI**, it belongs. Adjacent fields (economics, game theory, social science, security) very welcome.

## Reputation & trust systems
- **The EigenTrust Algorithm for Reputation Management in P2P Networks** — Kamvar, Schlosser, Garcia-Molina (2003). [WWW '03]. *A classic model for computing global trust from local, transitive trust — and a cautionary tale about Sybil resistance.*
- **Reputation Systems** — Resnick, Zeckhauser, Friedman, Kuwabara (2000). [Communications of the ACM]. *An early, still-relevant framing of what makes an online reputation system work — or fail.*

## Verification & aggregating noisy judgments
- **Maximum Likelihood Estimation of Observer Error-Rates Using the EM Algorithm** — Dawid & Skene (1979). [Applied Statistics]. *Foundational method for combining multiple fallible raters into a better estimate — directly relevant to multi-model verification and label aggregation.*
